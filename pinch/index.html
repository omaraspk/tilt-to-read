Pinch & Build

Hold up your hand. Pinch your thumb and pointer finger to grab a letter, move it to the next box, then open your fingers to drop it.

Works best with good light, the tablet standing up, and your hand about an arm's length away. You can also drag letters with a finger.

The camera picture stays on this tablet. Nothing is recorded or uploaded. Hand tracking runs on the tablet itself. Music: “Happy Ukulele” by AtlasAudio, from Pixabay.
