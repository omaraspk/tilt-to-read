Balloon Pop 🎈

Read the word at the top. Pop every balloon with that word using your pointer finger. Pop 5 and you get a new word! Golden balloons give bonus points and extra time.

Works best with good light, the tablet standing up, and the child about an arm's length away. You can also tap the balloons with a finger.

The camera picture stays on this tablet. Nothing is recorded or uploaded. Hand tracking runs on the tablet itself. Music: “Happy Ukulele” by AtlasAudio, from Pixabay.
